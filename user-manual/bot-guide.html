Expense Tracker Bot

A Telegram bot for tracking money by text: expenses, income, budgets, recurring bills, goals, debts — even a photo of a receipt or a voice note. This is the full command set, in the order you'll actually need it.

🤖 Platform: Telegram DM ⌨️ No command-menu autocomplete — type commands in full 💱 Default currency: ₹ INR

⚠ Start here — the bot's own /help leaves this out

Every money command needs an account to already exist. Try /add 500 lunch before creating one, and it fails with "No account found for user." The bot's help text shows /add like it works immediately after /start — it doesn't. Do this instead:

  1. Send the bot any message (or /start). This silently creates your account and seeds 11 default categories — nothing else works before this.
  2. Create an account: /account add Wallet cash. Every command below acts on your oldest account automatically — there's no way to pick a different one per-command.
  3. Now /add 500 lunch works. Everything from here on is optional, in any order.

Getting started

The bare minimum to exist as a user.

/start

Registers you and seeds 11 default categories (Food & Dining, Transportation, Shopping, Entertainment, Bills & Utilities, Healthcare, Education — expense; Salary, Freelance, Investment, Other Income — income). Safe to send again, it won't duplicate anything.

/start
Welcome to Expense Tracker Bot! Use /help to see available commands.

/help

Bot's built-in command reference. Treat this page as the complete version — the in-bot one is missing several commands and doesn't mention photo or voice input.

Accounts do this first

Every transaction, recurrence, and report reads or writes against an account. You need at least one before anything money-related works.

/account add <name> <type> [currency] [balance]

Creates an account. Currency defaults to INR, starting balance to 0.

typechecking · savings · credit · cash · investment · other
/account add Wallet cash
✅ Account created!
Name: Wallet
Type: cash
Currency: INR
Balance: ₹0.00

/account list

Lists every account and its current balance.

/account list
💳 Your accounts:
• Wallet (cash) [INR]
  Balance: 0.00

/account transfer <from_id> <to_id> <amount>

Moves money between two of your own accounts. Balance-checked — fails if the source can't cover it.

/account transfer 1 2 500
✅ Transfer complete!
Wallet → Bank: ₹500.00

/account delete <account_id>

Deletes an account. Get the id from /account list.

/account delete 2
🗑️ Account deleted.

Expenses & income

The daily-use commands. All three accept flexible amount formats — see the note below.

/add <amount> <description>

Records an expense against your default (oldest) account.

/add 500 lunch
✅ Expense recorded!
Amount: ₹500
Description: lunch

+<amount> <description>

Income shorthand — no slash. Fastest way to log income on the fly.

+1000 freelance payment
✅ Income recorded!
Amount: ₹1000
Description: freelance payment

/income <amount> <description>

Same as +, spelled out — identical result.

/income 2000 salary
✅ Income recorded!
Amount: ₹2000
Description: salary
💡 Amount formats accepted: 500, ₹500, 50 USD, 50$, 1,000 (commas OK). Bare numbers default to INR. Without a currency-conversion key configured on the server, non-INR amounts are stored at face value — no real exchange-rate math happens.

Reports & export

/today

Today's income, expense, and net — broken down by category.

/monthly [month] [year]

Monthly summary: totals, top-5 expense categories, transaction count. Defaults to the current month.

/monthly 8 2026

/export [start-date] [end-date]

Sends a CSV file of your transactions as a Telegram document. Dates as YYYY-MM-DD; defaults to today.

/export 2026-09-01 2026-09-30

Budgets

/budget <category> <amount> <month> <year>

Sets a monthly spending cap for one category.

/budget Food 5000 9 2026

/budgetstatus [month] [year]

Shows every budgeted category against actual spend, color-coded 🟢 under, 🟡 close, 🔴 over.

Recurring transactions

A background worker checks every minute and auto-posts anything due — as a real transaction against your default account.

/recur add <amount> <description> <expense|income> every <n> <day|week|month> [start] [end]

Schedules a repeating transaction. Swap every <n> <unit> for cron <expression> if you need finer control.

/recur add 1200 rent expense every 1 month

/recur list

Shows every active recurrence and its next run.

/recur delete <id>

Cancels a recurrence.

Reminders

/reminder on [HH:MM]

Daily nudge to log expenses. 24-hour time, defaults to 21:00.

/reminder on 20:30
⏰ What you get, daily: "Reminder: It's time to log yesterday's expenses. Use /add or + to record income/expenses."

/reminder off

Turns the daily nudge off.

Goals

/goal set <name> <target_amount>

Creates a savings goal.

/goal set Vacation 50000

/goal list

Shows every goal with % progress toward target.

/goal progress <goal_id> <amount>

Adds an amount toward a goal's saved total.

/goal progress 1 5000

/goal delete <goal_id>

Removes a goal.

Debts

Tracks money owed between you and other people — separate from your account balances.

/debt lend <person> <amount> [note]

Records money you lent out.

/debt lend Raj 2000 movie tickets

/debt borrow <person> <amount> [note]

Records money you borrowed.

/debt settle <id> <amount>

Pays down a debt — partial amounts are tracked, so you can settle in installments.

/debt list

Every open debt, ⏳ outstanding or ✅ settled.

/debt delete <id>

Removes a debt record entirely.

Photo & voice input

Not slash commands — just send the bot media directly. Both end the same way: a confirmation card you approve before anything is saved.

📷 Send a photo of a receipt

OCR reads the amount, date, and a description guess off the receipt. Always saved as an expense.

🧾 OCR Result
Amount: ₹1,234.50
Save this expense?
✅ Yes, save ❌ No, cancel

🎙️ Send a voice note

Transcribed automatically. Say a number that starts with "plus" or write + conceptually and it's treated as income; otherwise expense.

🎙️ Voice Transcription
"500 rupees for groceries"
Save this as an expense?
✅ Yes, save ❌ No, cancel
⚠ Both are held in memory only until you tap a button. Cancel, get distracted, or the bot restarts — the pending entry is gone, nothing was saved.

Good to know

  • No command menu. There's no Telegram autocomplete list for this bot — every command has to be typed out in full, exactly as shown above.
  • "Default account" = oldest account. Every command that touches money uses whichever account you created first. There's no per-command way to target a different one.
  • Categories are fixed. The 11 seeded at /start are all you get — new transactions attach to the first existing category of matching type, nothing gets auto-created per description.
  • Currency is mostly cosmetic. The bot parses USD/$ amounts fine, but without a conversion key configured server-side, it stores the number as typed — 50 USD and ₹50 are stored as the same "50."
  • Recurring transactions post silently. The worker runs every minute; a due recurrence just appears as a normal transaction — no message announces it.
